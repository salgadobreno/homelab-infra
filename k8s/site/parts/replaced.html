What this replaced

From a record, not a reading. The system described here no longer exists, so nothing in it can be checked — unlike everything above.

Until 22 August 2026 this site was three Docker containers on the hypervisor itself: nginx, a hit counter, and redis holding two keys. It ran for sixteen days and served 69 visitors.

The hit counter was never moved into the cluster, and why not is the clearest statement of what was wrong with it: the image had been built on that host and pushed nowhere, so there was no copy for a cluster to pull. It existed in exactly one place, with no way to reproduce it. Nothing above this has that property.
