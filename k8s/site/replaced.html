What this replaced

From a record, not a reading. The system described here no longer exists, so nothing in it can be checked against anything — unlike every fact above.

Until 22 August 2026 this site was three Docker containers on the hypervisor itself, started by docker compose and kept alive by a systemd unit: nginx:latest serving the page, a hit counter, and redis:7-alpine holding two keys.

It worked for sixteen days and served 69 visitors, 6 of them returning. That was the entire persistent state of it.

The hit counter was not moved into the cluster, and the reason is the clearest statement of what was wrong with the arrangement: the image had been built on that host and never pushed anywhere, so there was no copy for a cluster to pull. The running service existed in exactly one place, with no way to reproduce it elsewhere. That is not a deployment; it is a pet.

The arrangement above does not have that property. Every image named there is pulled from somewhere public, and the whole arrangement is rebuilt from an empty hypervisor in about three minutes by a single command.
